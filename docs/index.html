City vs. county population, 1800–2020

The 100 largest cities in the contiguous U.S. (2020 Census): population inside city limits, population of the surrounding county, and the city's share of its county, from decennial census counts.

Each year uses the cities with both a city and a county count that year (n under each year). Unweighted: simple mean of city shares. Population-weighted: shares weighted by county population, i.e. total city population ÷ total county population (the ratio of the two bars).

Definitions

City population: Stanford CESTA compilation of U.S. Census Bureau place counts (1800–2010) and the 2020 Census. County population: Census county file 1900–2020 (van Leuven) and Forstall (1996), Population of States and Counties of the United States: 1790–1990, for 1800–1890. The comparison county is the county (or county-equivalent) containing the city at each census; where it changes, the city chart marks the year with a dashed rule. Cities extending into other counties are compared with their principal county, which overstates the share somewhat. CESTA records places below the 2,500 reporting threshold as 0; these are shown as missing.
